Privacy

Recipe Box, the Android app (com.spagoodlers.recipebox) and this website. Last updated 20 October 2026.

Recipe Box doesn’t collect any information about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you do in Recipe Box is sent to us; we have no server that could receive it.

What stays on your device

Your recipes, books, shopping list, meal plans and settings are kept on your device: in the app’s own storage on Android, or in your browser’s storage on the website. Uninstalling the app or clearing the site’s data removes them.

Sync and backups

If you turn on sync or save a backup, Recipe Box writes your recipes to a file you choose: in a folder on your device or in a service such as Google Drive or Dropbox. That file is handled by you and that service under its own privacy policy. We never see it.

Recipe websites

When you search for a recipe or open one from a website, Recipe Box loads that site, as a browser would. Those sites may set cookies or collect information under their own privacy policies. Recipe Box keeps only the recipe you choose to save.

On this website, a search goes from your browser straight to the recipe sites you’ve switched on (and only those), with what you typed; opening a recipe asks that site for it the same way. Nothing passes through us.

The “Send to Recipe Box” bookmark reads the recipe from the page you’re on and opens it here, in the web address itself. It goes from that page to this one in your browser, and nowhere else.

If whoever serves you Recipe Box has set a “Fetch through” address (Setup → Your recipes), recipe pages you import are fetched through that address, under that service’s own policy. Out of the box there is none.

Reading aloud and printing

Reading steps aloud uses your device’s own text-to-speech; printing and saving PDFs use your device’s own printing. Recipe Box sends nothing to us for either.

Permissions

The Android app asks only for internet access, to load recipe websites. Files are opened only when you pick them.

Children

Recipe Box collects no information from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact

If this changes, this page will say so first. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
